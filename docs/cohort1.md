Below are **20 example cohort definitions** spanning a variety of conditions, procedures, and medication-based phenotypes, all oriented toward **predicting future events** (similar to your hip replacement example). Each cohort includes a brief rationale, potential **inclusion** and **exclusion** criteria, and relevant **code types** (ICD-10-CM for diagnoses, CPT/HCPCS for procedures/services, and GPI for medications). Where age and gender are relevant, they are noted. All are intended as **draft outlines**—your clinical team can refine the exact codes, time windows, and clinical details.

## 1. Future Need for Total Knee Replacement (TKR)

**Rationale:**  
Similar in spirit to the hip replacement example, this cohort aims to identify patients who may require a knee replacement in the near future based on prior clinical indicators.

- **Inclusion Criteria:**  
  - Presence of ICD-10-CM codes indicative of osteoarthritis of the knee (e.g., *M17.xx*).  
  - Relevant CPT/HCPCS codes in historical claims showing conservative knee treatments (e.g., *intra-articular injections*, knee arthroscopy if any) or prior physical therapy sessions.  
  - Age typically over 50 (adjust as clinically appropriate).

- **Exclusion Criteria:**  
  - History of any knee replacement procedure (e.g., CPT *27447* or *27446*) in the past (already performed).  
  - Severe comorbidities that contraindicate surgery (e.g., advanced cancer codes).

## 2. Likelihood of Coronary Artery Bypass Graft (CABG)

**Rationale:**  
Identify patients who may need a CABG in the near future based on prior cardiac issues and current severity of coronary artery disease.

- **Inclusion Criteria:**  
  - ICD-10-CM codes for coronary artery disease (e.g., *I25.xx*) or angina.  
  - Evidence of prior percutaneous coronary interventions (e.g., CPT *92928*), but with new/worsening symptoms.  
  - Possibly on GPI-coded cardiovascular medications (e.g., statins, beta-blockers) indicating advanced management.

- **Exclusion Criteria:**  
  - Prior CABG surgery (e.g., CPT *33510*–*33536* range).  
  - End-stage comorbidities that preclude surgery (e.g., ICD-10-CM codes for advanced dementia, hospice care).

---

## 3. Risk of Progression to Advanced Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) Requiring Dialysis

**Rationale:**  
Identify patients who may progress to end-stage renal disease in the foreseeable future.

- **Inclusion Criteria:**  
  - ICD-10-CM codes for CKD stages 3–4 (e.g., *N18.3*, *N18.4*).  
  - Rising trend of serum creatinine or eGFR drop (if lab data available; if not, look for frequent nephrology visits or relevant CPT/HCPCS codes).  
  - Use of GPI-coded ACE inhibitors/ARBs or other renal-protective agents (common in CKD management).

- **Exclusion Criteria:**  
  - Already on dialysis (HCPCS codes *G0257*, *S9339* or CPT codes *90935*–*90937*).  
  - Kidney transplant recipients (ICD-10-CM Z94.0 or CPT *50360*–*50365*).

## 4. Future Initiation of Biologic Therapy for Rheumatoid Arthritis

**Rationale:**  
Identify patients with rheumatoid arthritis who are likely to escalate to biologic disease-modifying anti-rheumatic drugs (DMARDs).

- **Inclusion Criteria:**  
  - ICD-10-CM codes for rheumatoid arthritis (e.g., *M05.xx*, *M06.xx*).  
  - Evidence of prior conventional DMARD use (e.g., methotrexate) from GPI codes or relevant medication codes.  
  - Indicators of moderate-to-severe disease activity (e.g., more frequent rheumatology visits).

- **Exclusion Criteria:**  
  - Already on biologics (e.g., GPI codes for adalimumab, etanercept, etc.).  
  - Active serious infections or contraindications to biologic therapy (e.g., tuberculosis ICD codes).

## 5. Potential Transition to Insulin Therapy for Type 2 Diabetes (T2DM)

**Rationale:**  
Identify T2DM patients who may require insulin (based on poor glycemic control or advanced disease).

- **Inclusion Criteria:**  
  - ICD-10-CM for T2DM (e.g., *E11.xx*).  
  - Current use of oral hypoglycemics (GPI codes for metformin, sulfonylureas, etc.).  
  - Repeated high A1c levels (if lab data is available) or frequent diabetes-related outpatient visits (CPT for diabetes management visits).

- **Exclusion Criteria:**  
  - Already on insulin therapy (GPI codes for insulin).  
  - Type 1 diabetes ICD-10-CM (*E10.xx*).

## 6. Risk of Opioid Initiation or Long-Term Opioid Use

**Rationale:**  
Identify patients with chronic pain who may escalate to long-term opioid therapy.

- **Inclusion Criteria:**  
  - ICD-10-CM codes for chronic pain (e.g., *G89.xx*) or specific chronic conditions (e.g., fibromyalgia *M79.7*, chronic low back pain *M54.5*).  
  - Documented use of non-opioid analgesics (e.g., NSAIDs from GPI codes).  
  - Multiple pain management visits (CPT codes for physical therapy, pain management consults).

- **Exclusion Criteria:**  
  - Active or prior long-term opioid prescriptions (GPI codes for opioids over a certain duration).  
  - Documented opioid misuse or substance use disorder (ICD-10-CM *F11.xx*).

## 7. Future Use of Antidepressant Medications for Major Depression

**Rationale:**  
Identify patients who may escalate to pharmacotherapy for depression.

- **Inclusion Criteria:**  
  - ICD-10-CM codes for major depressive disorder (e.g., *F32.xx*, *F33.xx*).  
  - Documented psychotherapy visits or mild depression not yet on medication.  
  - Possibly elevated PHQ-9 scores if available (otherwise rely on frequent mental health visits).

- **Exclusion Criteria:**  
  - Already on antidepressants (GPI codes for SSRIs, SNRIs, etc.).  
  - Bipolar disorder (ICD-10-CM *F31.xx*) to exclude other medication regimens.

## 8. Future Onset of Acute Myocardial Infarction (AMI) Requiring Intervention

**Rationale:**  
Identify high-risk patients (based on existing cardiovascular issues) who might experience an MI requiring emergent care or stent placement.

- **Inclusion Criteria:**  
  - ICD-10-CM for unstable angina or prior mild ischemic episodes (e.g., *I20.xx*).  
  - Elevated risk factors (hyperlipidemia, hypertension, diabetes codes).  
  - Possibly GPI-coded cardiovascular medications (statins, antiplatelets) indicating advanced management of CAD.

- **Exclusion Criteria:**  
  - Recent AMI within a short lookback window (since they are in acute or immediate post-acute phase).  
  - Palliative/end-of-life codes.

## 9. Future Demand for Colonoscopy Screening in Average-Risk Adults

**Rationale:**  
Identify patients who are likely due for a screening colonoscopy based on age and prior screening intervals.

- **Inclusion Criteria:**  
  - Age 45–75 (typical average-risk screening range).  
  - No colonoscopy CPT codes (e.g., *45378*) in the last 10 years (or 5 years if prior findings).  
  - No high-risk ICD-10-CM codes requiring more frequent screenings (e.g., familial polyposis).

- **Exclusion Criteria:**  
  - Known colon polyps with polypectomy in the last 3 years (would be a different screening schedule).  
  - Severe comorbid conditions limiting life expectancy.

## 10. Future Requirement for Bariatric Surgery

**Rationale:**  
Identify patients with obesity who might qualify for and eventually pursue surgical interventions (e.g., gastric bypass).

- **Inclusion Criteria:**  
  - ICD-10-CM codes for obesity (e.g., *E66.xx*).  
  - Documented weight management visits or dietary counseling (CPT codes like *97802*, *97803*).  
  - Potential comorbid conditions (hypertension, T2DM) indicating metabolic syndrome.

- **Exclusion Criteria:**  
  - Prior bariatric surgery (CPT *43644*–*43645*, *43770*–*43775*).  
  - Contraindications to surgery (severe heart failure, advanced cancer).

## 11. Future Initiation of Biologics for Inflammatory Bowel Disease (IBD)

**Rationale:**  
Identify IBD (Crohn’s or Ulcerative Colitis) patients who may require escalation to biologic therapy.

- **Inclusion Criteria:**  
  - ICD-10-CM codes for Crohn’s disease (*K50.xx*) or ulcerative colitis (*K51.xx*).  
  - Evidence of moderate or severe flares (frequent gastroenterology visits, steroid use from GPI codes).  
  - Failure or intolerance of standard therapies (e.g., 5-ASA drugs).

- **Exclusion Criteria:**  
  - Already on biologic therapy (e.g., infliximab, adalimumab via GPI codes).  
  - Coexisting conditions that contraindicate biologics (e.g., certain infections).

## 12. Future Progression of Diabetic Retinopathy Requiring Laser or Surgical Intervention

**Rationale:**  
Identify diabetic patients likely to progress to advanced retinopathy.

- **Inclusion Criteria:**  
  - ICD-10-CM codes for diabetic retinopathy (*E11.3xx*).  
  - Ophthalmology visits or CPT codes for retinal imaging (e.g., *92250*).  
  - Poorly controlled diabetes (evidenced by multiple medication changes or frequent visits).

- **Exclusion Criteria:**  
  - Previous laser photocoagulation (CPT *67210*, *67228*) or vitrectomy for retinopathy.  
  - Loss to follow-up (no visits in the past year).

## 13. Likelihood of Future Immunotherapy for Cancer Patients

**Rationale:**  
Identify patients with certain malignancies who may progress to advanced disease requiring immunotherapy (e.g., checkpoint inhibitors).

- **Inclusion Criteria:**  
  - ICD-10-CM codes for malignant neoplasms (e.g., *Cxx.x*).  
  - Already completed or failing standard chemotherapy (GPI codes for chemo agents).  
  - Possible genetic or molecular markers indicating immunotherapy is a next step (if data available).

- **Exclusion Criteria:**  
  - Already on immunotherapy agents (e.g., PD-1, PD-L1 inhibitors).  
  - Limited life expectancy or hospice (ICD-10-CM Z51.5, etc.).

## 15. Future Joint Injection Therapy for Advanced Osteoarthritis

**Rationale:**  
Identify patients with advanced osteoarthritis who are likely candidates for intra-articular injection (e.g., hyaluronic acid, corticosteroids).

- **Inclusion Criteria:**  
  - ICD-10-CM codes for osteoarthritis of major joints (e.g., *M17.x*, *M19.x*).  
  - Documented pain scores or physical therapy attempts.  
  - No prior injection therapy or it’s been >6 months since last injection.

- **Exclusion Criteria:**  
  - Active infection in the joint (e.g., septic arthritis codes *M00.xx*).  
  - Patients who have already scheduled total replacement surgery.

## 16. Future Need for Advanced Imaging (MRI/CT) in Neurological Disorders

**Rationale:**  
Identify patients with neurological symptoms (e.g., chronic headaches, epilepsy, suspected MS) who might escalate to advanced imaging.

- **Inclusion Criteria:**

## 18. Risk of Future Acute Exacerbation in COPD Patients

**Rationale:**  
Identify chronic obstructive pulmonary disease patients likely to have an acute exacerbation requiring hospitalization.

- **Inclusion Criteria:**  
  - ICD-10-CM codes for COPD (*J44.xx*).  
  - Frequent or escalating use of rescue inhalers, GPI-coded inhaled steroids/long-acting bronchodilators.  
  - At least one prior COPD exacerbation (ER visit, CPT for acute services) in the past 12 months.

- **Exclusion Criteria:**  
  - End-stage respiratory failure on home ventilation.  
  - Terminal comorbidities or hospice codes.

# Next Steps

   - Each cohort’s ICD/CPT/HCPCS/GPI codes should be **validated and possibly expanded** by your clinical team to ensure they capture the correct patient population.

2. **Define Time Windows**  
   - Decide how far back (lookback window) for inclusion criteria and how far forward you want to predict (e.g., next 6 months, 12 months).

3. **Set Additional Clinical Criteria**  
   - Incorporate lab results, vitals, or imaging findings if your data pipeline supports them.  
   - For example, to identify risk of dialysis, eGFR trends would be crucial if available.

4. **Outcome Labeling**  
   - Clearly define how you label someone as a “positive” case (e.g., the appearance of a CPT code for the surgery/procedure within 6 or 12 months after the index date).
